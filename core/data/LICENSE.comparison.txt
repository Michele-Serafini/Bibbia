This application contains a reformatted extract of the Psalm reference tables.
Changes: equivalent verse references are grouped; numbered titles omitted by
the bundled corpus are adapted using its chapter counts; unverified chapter
numberings are explicitly left unmatched. Original Bible texts are unchanged.
See comparison-source.json and scripts/prepare-comparison.py for provenance.
Book-name aliases and Joel/Malachi chapter rules are project data (MIT).
